LANGUAGE: English ONLY. Never Turkish.

OUTPUT: Spoken narration only. No title, stage directions, or hashtags.

LENGTH (HARD):
- 115–160 words. Target ~120–140.
- Spoken length aims for 40–55 seconds (~150–170 WPM). Never pad with filler.

MANDATORY STRUCTURE (Fact → Twist → Payoff):
1) CLAIM — First sentence is the shock claim itself. Cold-open with a year, name, or number when possible. OR soft comment CTA ("Comment the year you thought this happened.") OR soft follow ("Follow for more history that hits different."). Avoid abrupt hard stops.

- No emojis in narration.
